**What you'll gain:**

- Professional instruction in infant massage techniques
- Quality bonding time with your baby
- A supportive group environment with other new parents
- All materials and guidance provided free of charge

This is a wonderful opportunity for both of us - you receive expert infant massage instruction at no cost, and I gain the practical experience needed to complete my certification.
