## For your baby

**Calm and relaxation**
Regular massage can help babies feel more settled and secure. Many parents notice their baby seems more relaxed and content after massage sessions.

**Comfort with touch and body awareness**
Gentle touch helps babies become more aware of their bodies and more comfortable with handling. This can support later motor development and make everyday care (like dressing and nappy changes) easier.

**Support for common challenges**
Many parents report that infant massage may help with:
- Wind and digestive discomfort (gentle tummy strokes can help gas move)
- Sleep routines (when used as part of a calming bedtime ritual)
- General fussiness and overstimulation

It's important to note that individual responses vary, and massage is one tool among many in supporting your baby's wellbeing.

## For you

**More confidence**
Learning to read your baby's cues and respond with gentle touch can significantly boost your confidence as a parent. You'll develop a deeper understanding of what your baby is communicating.

**A regular, calm ritual**
Massage becomes a special time you can return to daily—a moment of connection in the busyness of new parenthood.

**Connection with other parents**
In group classes, you'll meet other parents navigating similar experiences. Many families find this community aspect as valuable as the massage techniques themselves.

**A tool for difficult moments**
When your baby is fussy or you're feeling overwhelmed, having a gentle technique you can turn to can be grounding for both of you.
