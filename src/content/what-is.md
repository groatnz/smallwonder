Infant massage is the intentional, gentle, structured touch of a baby's body by a parent or primary caregiver, usually using the hands with or without oil. It combines elements of:

- Gentle stroking and holding
- Rhythmic movements and stretching
- Soothing voice, eye contact, and responsive interaction

The core idea is not "treatment done to a baby" but communication with the baby through touch, supporting bonding, comfort, and healthy development.

## What infant massage is not

- Not deep tissue massage
- Not a replacement for medical care
- Not forced if baby is upset
- Not a one-size-fits-all routine
- Not something done to your baby by someone else

Infant massage is always baby-led, respectful of your baby's cues, and something you learn to do yourself as a parent.
