# Sora Logo Generation Prompt for SmallWonder

## Primary Prompt

**A simple, minimalist logo animation for SmallWonder infant massage: gentle flowing lines forming a soft silhouette of a parent's hand cradling a small baby, rendered in soft sage green (#A8D5BA) and warm rose (#F7D8E1) on a warm sand background (#F4E3C1). The lines are organic and fluid, suggesting gentle touch and connection. The design is clean, modern, and works at small sizes. The animation shows the lines gently flowing and connecting, like a gentle massage stroke, then settling into the final logo mark. Soft, warm lighting, no harsh edges, peaceful and nurturing feeling.**

## Alternative Prompts (Variations)

### Option 1: Abstract Connection
**Minimalist logo animation: two soft, flowing curves that gently meet and connect, representing parent and baby bonding through touch. Colors: sage green and soft rose on warm sand background. The curves flow together like gentle hands meeting, then settle into a simple, elegant mark. Clean, modern, scalable design with soft edges and warm, nurturing atmosphere.**

### Option 2: Hand and Heart
**Simple logo animation: a gentle, abstract representation of a caring hand with a small heart or circle at the center, symbolizing the tender connection between parent and baby. Soft sage green and rose colors on warm sand. The hand shape forms from flowing lines that settle into place. Minimalist, clean, works well at small sizes. Peaceful, warm, and nurturing feeling.**

### Option 3: Circular Connection
**Minimalist circular logo animation: two soft, interlocking shapes within a circle, representing the gentle bond between parent and baby. One shape in sage green, one in soft rose, on warm sand background. The shapes gently flow together and settle. Clean, modern, timeless design. Soft edges, no harsh lines, peaceful and calming.**

## Technical Notes for Sora

- **Duration**: 3-5 seconds (can be looped)
- **Style**: Minimalist, clean, modern
- **Colors**: Sage green (#A8D5BA), Soft rose (#F7D8E1), Warm sand (#F4E3C1)
- **Mood**: Gentle, nurturing, peaceful, warm, calm
- **Animation**: Subtle, flowing movement that settles into final logo mark
- **Scalability**: Design should work at small sizes (favicon, social media profile)

## Brand Context

SmallWonder is an infant massage instruction business that teaches parents gentle, baby-led massage techniques to support bonding, relaxation, and confidence. The logo should reflect:
- Gentle touch and connection
- Parent-baby bonding
- Calm, non-clinical approach
- Warm, nurturing atmosphere
- Professional yet approachable

## Usage Recommendation

For a static logo, consider extracting a single frame from the animation or using an image generation tool (like DALL-E, Midjourney, or Stable Diffusion) with a similar prompt but without the animation aspect.
