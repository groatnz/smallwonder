Infant massage is always baby-led, respectful of your baby's cues, and something you learn to do yourself as a parent.

- Gentle massage strokes
- Rhythmic movements and stretching

The core idea is communication with the baby through touch, supporting bonding, comfort, and healthy development.

## What infant massage is not

- Not deep tissue massage
- Not a replacement for medical care
- Not a one-size-fits-all routine
