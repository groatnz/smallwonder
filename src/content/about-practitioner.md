Millie is a certified infant massage instructor based on Waiheke Island, passionate about supporting new parents and their babies through the gentle practice of infant massage.

With a background in supporting families during the early months, Millie understands the challenges and joys of new parenthood. She completed her training through the International Association of Infant Massage (IAIM), learning evidence-based techniques that have been taught to parents worldwide for decades.

Millie's approach is warm, patient, and completely baby-led. She believes that every parent has the capacity to learn these techniques, and that massage is a beautiful way to strengthen the bond between you and your baby while supporting their development and comfort.

Whether you're feeling confident or overwhelmed, whether your baby is calm or colicky, Millie creates a safe, non-judgmental space where you can learn at your own pace and your baby's pace.
